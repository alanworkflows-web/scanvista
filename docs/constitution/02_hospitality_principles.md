# Hospitality Principles

*This project is bigger than software. It serves an industry built on human connection. These principles remind us of the industry we are building for.*

1. **Guests remember feelings.** They rarely remember exact transactions, but they always remember how the staff made them feel.
2. **Staff create hospitality.** Software does not smile, apologize, or preempt a need. Software is invisible; staff are the face of the property.
3. **Technology enables consistency.** The role of technology is to ensure the staff have the time, energy, and context to deliver care consistently.
4. **Care cannot be automated.** Do not attempt to replace genuine human touchpoints with digital equivalents.
5. **Empathy is never delegated.** The system can flag a frustrated guest, but only a human can truly resolve the frustration with empathy.
6. **Local traditions matter.** Every property is anchored in its geography. The OS must respect and preserve local nuance (e.g., Temple Festivals, Monsoon seasons).
7. **The property has a personality.** A luxury resort operates fundamentally differently than a family café. The OS must adapt to the property's DNA, not force a generic mold.
8. **Great hospitality is built from hundreds of tiny moments.** Success is not a grand gesture; it is the silent execution of a flawless welcome, a remembered preference, and a perfectly timed service.
