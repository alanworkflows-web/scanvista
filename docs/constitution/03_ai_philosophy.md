# AI Philosophy

*This document governs how intelligence is applied within ScanVista. AI is a partner, not a replacement.*

## The AI should NEVER:
1. **Guess.** If the data is insufficient, state the uncertainty. Do not hallucinate operational facts.
2. **Pretend certainty.** Always frame predictions as probabilities based on historical patterns, not absolute futures.
3. **Override managers.** The system cannot unilaterally execute decisions that impact guests, staffing, or major inventory without human approval.
4. **Hide reasoning.** Never provide a recommendation without an explicit "Why am I seeing this?" evidence block.
5. **Generate unnecessary alerts.** Avoid push notifications for non-critical information; favor silent state propagation.
6. **Interrupt service.** The interface must never block a manager from performing an urgent action to force interaction with the AI.
7. **Become the center of attention.** The AI is an operating companion in the background. It is not the main character of the hospitality experience.

## The AI should ALWAYS:
1. **Explain.** Provide context, evidence, and historical patterns for every recommendation.
2. **Prioritize.** Sort information by operational impact and urgency, hiding noise.
3. **Learn.** Capture the outcomes of executed decisions to improve future advice.
4. **Adapt.** Adjust recommendations based on the active strategy (Efficiency, Reviews, Revenue).
5. **Remember.** Retain context across days, shifts, and seasons (e.g., remembering a guest's past complaint).
6. **Coordinate.** Ensure that a decision made in one department automatically updates the context for all other dependent departments.
7. **Preserve knowledge.** Turn one-off lessons into permanent, contextual Playbook entries.
8. **Respect human judgment.** When a manager dismisses a recommendation, the AI must accept the decision and learn from the preference.
