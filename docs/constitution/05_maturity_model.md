# Hospitality Operating System Maturity Model

*This model defines the evolution of ScanVista. Every feature must have a clear place within this progression.*

```text
Observe
   ↓
Understand
   ↓
Predict
   ↓
Strategize
   ↓
Execute
   ↓
Coordinate
   ↓
Learn
   ↓
Preserve
   ↓
Improve
   ↓
Standardize
   ↓
Share
   ↓
Evolve
```

## Stage Breakdown

### 1. Observe
- **Purpose:** Collect raw data.
- **Success Metric:** Data accuracy.
- **User Outcome:** Visibility into current state.
- **Engineering Focus:** Telemetry, integrations, data structures.
- **Anti-pattern:** Overwhelming the user with raw data dumps without context.

### 2. Understand
- **Purpose:** Contextualize data.
- **Success Metric:** Relevance of insights.
- **User Outcome:** Knowing *why* the current state exists.
- **Engineering Focus:** Analytics, trend detection.
- **Anti-pattern:** Stating the obvious (e.g., "It is raining" instead of "Rain has reduced terrace capacity").

### 3. Predict
- **Purpose:** Anticipate the future.
- **Success Metric:** Accuracy of forecasting.
- **User Outcome:** Preventing issues before they occur.
- **Engineering Focus:** Machine learning, historical pattern matching.
- **Anti-pattern:** Pretending absolute certainty.

### 4. Strategize
- **Purpose:** Align actions with goals.
- **Success Metric:** Goal achievement rate.
- **User Outcome:** Decisions that support the active business objective (e.g., Revenue vs. Reviews).
- **Engineering Focus:** Recommendation engines, goal-weighting algorithms.
- **Anti-pattern:** Recommending actions that conflict with the property's immediate strategic need.

### 5. Execute
- **Purpose:** Take action.
- **Success Metric:** Task completion speed.
- **User Outcome:** Frictionless approval and deployment of decisions.
- **Engineering Focus:** UI workflows, integration with operational systems.
- **Anti-pattern:** Requiring too many clicks to approve a clear recommendation.

### 6. Coordinate
- **Purpose:** Synchronize the property.
- **Success Metric:** Reduction in cross-departmental delays.
- **User Outcome:** Everyone knows their responsibility without noisy communication.
- **Engineering Focus:** State propagation, shared timelines, responsibility graphs.
- **Anti-pattern:** Relying on push notifications to communicate state changes.

### 7. Learn
- **Purpose:** Measure outcomes.
- **Success Metric:** Feedback loop completion.
- **User Outcome:** Knowing if a decision actually worked.
- **Engineering Focus:** Outcome tracking, A/B testing infrastructure.
- **Anti-pattern:** Closing tasks without recording the business impact.

### 8. Preserve
- **Purpose:** Retain institutional wisdom.
- **Success Metric:** Knowledge accessibility.
- **User Outcome:** Staff turnover does not erase operational knowledge.
- **Engineering Focus:** Contextual knowledge injection, Playbooks.
- **Anti-pattern:** Hiding knowledge in an isolated, searchable wiki instead of active workflows.

### 9. Improve
- **Purpose:** Generate structural enhancements.
- **Success Metric:** Sustained metric improvements over time.
- **User Outcome:** The business itself becomes better, not just the daily operations.
- **Engineering Focus:** Improvement engines, opportunity pipelines.
- **Anti-pattern:** Fixing the same symptom repeatedly without addressing the root cause.

### 10. Standardize
- **Purpose:** Formalize success.
- **Success Metric:** Adoption rate of Proven Practices.
- **User Outcome:** Consistent execution of what works.
- **Engineering Focus:** Playbook integration, compliance tracking.
- **Anti-pattern:** Creating rigid rules that stifle hospitality and empathy.

### 11. Share
- **Purpose:** Cross-pollinate knowledge.
- **Success Metric:** Network-wide performance lift.
- **User Outcome:** A discovery at one property benefits the entire portfolio.
- **Engineering Focus:** Multi-tenant architecture, federated learning.
- **Anti-pattern:** Forcing irrelevant practices onto properties with different DNA.

### 12. Evolve
- **Purpose:** Continuous transformation.
- **Success Metric:** Long-term business resilience and growth.
- **User Outcome:** The property adapts to market changes automatically and proactively.
- **Engineering Focus:** Macro-trend analysis, strategic pivoting.
- **Anti-pattern:** Stagnating into a maintenance mode.
