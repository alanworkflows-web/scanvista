# FAQ & Troubleshooting

## Guests can't see the menu?
Ensure you have created at least one Menu Category, and added Dishes to that category. Ensure those dishes are not marked "Out of Stock".

## QR Code is not scanning?
Ensure you printed the QR code directly from the ScanVista Dashboard. If you resize it manually in a photo editor, ensure you maintain the aspect ratio. We recommend printing at minimum 2x2 inches.

## My team member can't see the property?
Ensure you have invited them via the "Invite Team" button in the Settings panel. They must log in using the exact email address you invited.
