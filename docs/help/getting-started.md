# Getting Started with ScanVista

Welcome to ScanVista, the Operating System for Modern Hospitality. 

## Step 1: Claim Your Organization
When you first log in with your Google Account, we automatically create a secure workspace (Organization) for your team. You are the Owner of this organization.

## Step 2: Create a Property
Your Organization can manage multiple properties (Hotels, Resorts, Cafes). 
Click **"Add Property"** on the Manager Dashboard to get started. Give it a name and select the type of property.

## Step 3: Print Your QR Codes
Once your property is created, the system generates a unique **Digital Concierge** link. You can print the associated QR Code and place it in guest rooms, tables, or the lobby.
