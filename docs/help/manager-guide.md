# Manager Guide

As a Manager, you control what guests see and how your property operates.

## Menu Management
Navigate to the **Menu** tab on your Dashboard.
- **Categories**: Create categories like "Breakfast" or "Cocktails".
- **Dishes**: Add dishes, prices, allergen tags, and health tips. You can toggle "Out of Stock" instantly.

## Amenities
Navigate to the **Amenities** tab. Add your Gym, Pool, Spa, and set their Operating Hours. Guests will automatically see "Open Now" or "Closed" based on the property timezone.

## Intelligence & Analytics
Check your **Home Briefing** every morning for a summary of operations, or open **Founder HQ** for deeper cross-property analytics if you have multiple locations.
