# Staff Guide

As a staff member, ScanVista helps you stay on top of guest requests without the noise.

## Walk Mode
Use **Walk Mode** on your phone while rounding the property. It gives you a clean interface to report issues, update dish availability instantly, or log maintenance requests directly from the floor.

## Managing Guests
View the **Guests** tab to see who is arriving, currently staying, or checking out. 
You can generate personalized Digital Concierge links for VIP guests before they even arrive.
