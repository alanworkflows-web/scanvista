# Support & Contact

Need help? We're here for you.

## Email Support
For standard inquiries and non-urgent issues, email us at:
**support@scanvista.com**
(Response time: 12-24 hours)

## Emergency Escalation
If your system is completely down and guests cannot access your menu, call the Founder Escalation Line provided during your pilot onboarding.

## Feedback & Feature Requests
Have an idea to make ScanVista better? Use the "Feedback" widget on your Manager Dashboard to send thoughts directly to our product team.
