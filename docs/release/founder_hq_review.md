## Visual Identity

The Founder HQ strictly isolates its visual identity from the Manager OS.
- **Backgrounds:** Ivory/Surface colors (`#FCFAF8`)
- **Typography:** Serif for headings (`Playfair Display` or system serif), precise sans-serif for data.
- **Accents:** Restrained Gold/Emerald.
