## Role Walkthroughs

### 1. The Hotel Owner / GM (Manager OS)
- **Goal:** Understand property health and approve changes.
- **Experience:** The `ManagerLanding` and `ManagerHome` dashboards present a clean, distraction-free view. The Daily Briefing ("Here's what happened while you were sleeping") directly addresses GM anxieties.
- **Verdict:** ✅ Highly effective.

### 2. The Receptionist (Front Desk)
- **Goal:** Manage incoming guests.
- **Experience:** The `AdminCRM` and `ManagerGuests` views provide a clear Kanban-style or list view of arrivals. The one-click "Share Journey" button solves the immediate problem of onboarding guests.
- **Verdict:** ✅ Effective.

### 3. The Guest (Mobile View)
- **Goal:** Find information without downloading an app.
- **Experience:** The `GuestWelcome` page is lightning fast (cached on backend, optimized frontend). The typography and layout feel premium. Crucial information (WiFi, Reception phone) is immediately accessible.
- **Verdict:** ✅ Exceptional.

### 4. Housekeeping & Kitchen
- **Goal:** Update menus or check room status.
- **Experience:** `WalkMode` is designed for mobile-first, one-handed operation. `BrandStudio` allows instant menu publishing without technical skills.
- **Verdict:** ✅ Solid foundation.
